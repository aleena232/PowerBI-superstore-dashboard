This project was developed as part of a Data Analyst Internship task on Data Visualization and Storytelling.



The objective was to analyze Superstore sales data and create meaningful visualizations using Power BI to communicate business insights effectively.



## Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop

- Superstore Dataset (CSV)

- GitHub



## Dashboard Features

### Page 1: Sales Overview

- Total Sales KPI

- Total Profit KPI

- Total Orders KPI

- Total Quantity Sold KPI

- Sales by Category

- Profit by Category



### Page 2: Sales Trend Analysis

- Monthly Sales Trend



### Page 3: Regional & Product Analysis

- Sales by Region

- Top 10 Products by Sales



## Key Insights

- Total Sales exceeded 2.3 Million.

- Total Profit reached 286K.

- Technology category generated the highest sales and profit.

- West region recorded the highest sales.

- Top-selling products contributed significantly to overall revenue.



## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on Technology products.

- Increase investment in high-performing regions.

- Monitor sales trends to improve inventory planning.

- Ensure availability of top-selling products.
